# Найди пару

A memory-pairs browser game for children aged about 2–5, part of «Маленькие игры» at games.distantec.app (path `/pary/`). It uses the same engine, picture style and voice (Julia) as Буквоград and Цифроград. Everything is voiced; no reading is needed.

## How it plays
- The home screen has 6 tiles: «Звери», «Фрукты и овощи», «Транспорт», «Игрушки и вещи», «Небо и природа» and «Буква и картинка». The dots on a tile show finished levels, and the last played tile is highlighted. Tapping a tile opens its next level.
- Picture themes have 4 levels: 4 → 6 → 8 → 12 cards (2, 3, 4, 6 pairs). Pictures are picked at random from the theme on every play.
- On the first two levels all cards are shown face-up for a moment before they turn over.
- Tapping a card flips it in 3D and Julia names it («Кошка!» style: «Кот!»). A third tap is ignored while two cards are open.
- A match makes both cards hop, turn green and sparkle; Julia names the object. A mismatch flips back gently with a soft tick, never a sad sound.
- When the board is cleared, the cards do a wave, then the reward screen shows a sticker of one of the pictures, with buttons for the next level and home (side-by-side in landscape).
- «Буква и картинка»: pairs of a letter card and a picture that starts with that letter. Flipping a letter says its name (as in Буквоград); a match says «А — арбуз!». 7 levels cover 30 letters (all except Ы, Ь, Ъ, which have no word starting with them): 2, 3, 4, 6, 6, 6, 6 pairs.
- Card size is computed so the board fits the screen without scrolling on phone, small phone, phone landscape, tablet and tablet landscape.

Progress is kept in `localStorage` under `pary-v1`: `{done:{theme:{level:1}}, next:{theme:level}, last, pl}`. Broken saves fall back to a clean start.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole game, generated by `python3 src/build.py` from `src/template.html`, `src/game.js`, the shared engine parts (`src/shared_css.txt`, `src/shared_js.txt`, cut from Цифроград by `src/mk_template.py`), `src/pics*.js` and `src/game-data.json`.
- `src/data.py` – themes, levels, letter words and all voice texts; writes `game-data.json`, `clips.json` and `reuse.json`. Words and stress marks come from Буквоград's word list.
- `audio/*.mp3` – Yandex SpeechKit v3, voice `julia`, role `neutral`, speed 1.0; re-takes picked by artefact scan + ASR use 0.9–0.95 or a changed final mark (`tts/jl_overrides.json`, not shipped). Letter names (`name_*`) and praise (`ph_p1..7`) are reused from Буквоград, except `name_s` («Эс!»), voiced fresh for this game.

## Tests (Playwright)
`test/all.cjs` plays every level of every theme (`VP=phone|small|phoneL|tablet|tabletL`, `THEMES=…`, `SHOTS=prefix`), with a deliberate mismatch plus a quick third tap first, and checks overflow, console errors, HTTP errors and missing clips. `test/smoke.cjs` checks clips, pictures, save/restore and broken saves.
